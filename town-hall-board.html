Why a lamp at every seat

Because a board is a table where every seat counts the same. There is no light over the head of the table and no bigger lamp for anybody: the same small lamp, the same pool, at every place, down both sides. The room is dark around them, and what is said under them stays in the room. Nothing in here moves, at any setting.
