The Music Room

Just the music, with nothing to scroll past: the phonograph, the records, and a chair in the dark.

If you came for the songs and not for the rest of the yurt, this is the room. The phonograph is in here with both my playlists on it, and every song on them stands on a shelf as a record you can take out and put on. The window is open on the night, the candles never flicker, and the raven on the sill does not mind what you play. Nothing starts until you press it.

HOW LOUD DO YOU WANT IT?

Starts wherever your device says. In here MAX turns the record; nothing else moves unless you ask it to, below, and the candles stand still.

Faeries drifting between the candles, and ivy and mushrooms growing very slowly round the edges of the room. Each stays still until you press it, whatever the dial says, and pressing again clears it. Nothing is stored.

The Soundtrack

The sound of the yurt is a yarn of songs I keep coming back to, spun into two playlists: Faery Yurt’s Yarn, and Strange things will happen. Put one on and it runs from the top until you stop it; nothing plays before you press.

Faery Yurt’s Yarn and Strange things will happen and Symphrenia Chill, on YouTube, from my own channel.

Or take a record off a shelf: press its sleeve and it goes on the phonograph in place of the whole playlist and plays. Every sleeve says how long the record runs before you press it, nothing plays until you do, and taking it off puts the playlists back without starting either. A sleeve that opens on YouTube instead says so: its channel will not let other sites frame it.

The Music Book

Pull up a cushion and stay a while. This music book holds 51 playlists I've gathered over time, each one a little world of its own.

The overall feel is moonlit, dreamy and softly witchy, like candlelight in the yurt at dusk with rain on the roof. You'll find hazy dream pop and trip-hop from Portishead, Mazzy Star, Cocteau Twins, Lana Del Rey and Billie Eilish. There's faery folk and ethereal sound from AURORA, Kate Bush, Björk, Enya and the Mediæval Bæbes, plus forest ambience, gentle piano and classical calm for resting and drifting off.

There's a cosy side too: folk and cottagecore, Sunday country mornings, and jazz for an espresso martini or a Baileys. And when the fire needs stoking, there's grunge, 80s rock, power ballads to sing out loud and 90s dance to reminisce to.

Much of it is slow, textured and repetitive in the best way. It's music for soothing the senses, deep focus and getting happily lost. Each chapter has a link, so choose whatever suits your mood and press play.

Every playlist is a bar: press it and the whole playlist goes on right here, and runs until you stop it. Under each bar are its songs, in the playlist’s own order: press ▶ beside a song and the playlist plays from that song, in its own bar. There is also a link that opens it on YouTube. Nothing plays, and nothing is fetched from YouTube, until you press.
