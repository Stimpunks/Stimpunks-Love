Behind the Faery Yurt · through the door by the phonograph

The Music Room

Just the music, with nothing to scroll past: the phonograph, the records, and a chair in the dark.

If you came for the songs and not for the rest of the yurt, this is the room. The phonograph is in here with both my playlists on it, and every song on them stands on a shelf as a record you can take out and put on. The window is open on the night, the candles never flicker, and the raven on the sill does not mind what you play. Nothing starts until you press it.

HOW LOUD DO YOU WANT IT?

Starts wherever your device says. In here MAX turns the record; nothing else moves unless you ask it to, below, and the candles stand still.

Faeries drifting between the candles, and ivy and mushrooms growing very slowly round the edges of the room. Each stays still until you press it, whatever the dial says, and pressing again clears it. Nothing is stored.

The Soundtrack

The sound of the yurt is a yarn of songs I keep coming back to, spun into two playlists: Faery Yurt’s Yarn, and Strange things will happen. Put one on and it runs from the top until you stop it; nothing plays before you press.

Faery Yurt’s Yarn and Strange things will happen, on YouTube, from my own channel.

Or take a record off a shelf: press its sleeve and it goes on the phonograph in place of the whole playlist and plays. Every sleeve says how long the record runs before you press it, nothing plays until you do, and taking it off puts the playlists back without starting either. A sleeve that opens on YouTube instead says so: its channel will not let other sites frame it.
