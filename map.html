The Map

The whole street as a model in white card, standing on a cutting mat, and never finished.

How to read it

The street runs down the middle of the board from the treeline end at the top to the far end at the bottom, with the shopfronts standing on both sides of it in the order they stand on the front page. A small building behind a big one is a room behind a room. Past the treeline, off the top of the board, is the campground’s field with its pitches; past the last streetlight, off the bottom, is the road out of town with its turnings. Everything standing on the mat is a link: pick one up and it takes you there.

Follow Where am I? at the foot of any page on the street and the model opens at that page, with a flag on it saying you came from there.

Why nothing on it is painted

Every room on this street is its own world, with its own light, its own colours and its own typefaces, and no two of them alike. A model that tried to paint them all would be telling you they belonged to one set. So the whole thing is one white card under one lamp, the only colour on the table is the mat and the pins, and every room is left to be itself when you get there.

Nothing on it was placed by hand, either. The shopfronts come off the front page in the order they stand, the pitches off the campground’s own board and the turnings off the road’s own signs, and the gate to the garden stands halfway down however long the street happens to be. A room cannot open on this street without going up on the model, because the build refuses until it has.

Job marker — a spare pin with a white head, stuck in the cutting mat

You have found a job marker. The Adventurer’s Guild is a shopfront on the street, and it posts jobs that send you round these rooms. Take the code below to its board and that job is done. Nothing here is scored, nothing expires, and one is a perfectly good number of jobs to do.

Give me the answer

The answer is White card. Every room on the street has its own light and its own colours, and a model that painted each one would be saying they belonged to one set. So the whole model is one white card, and every room is left to be itself when you get there.

Taking the answer is a real way through this, not a lesser one. Nothing is watching and nothing is written down.
