    WHAT PENGUIN PEBBLING WILL NOT DO

    • It will not count what you gave. No tally, no total, no streak, nothing at the end. The locution is not about volume, and a number beside it would quietly make it about volume.
    • It will not require a pebble back. The neighbours bring you things on their own clock whether or not you have ever given them anything. That is the difference between pebbling and trading, and it is built as a timer rather than as a reward on purpose.
    • There is no wrong thing to give anybody. Every pebble is taken and every one gets something different said back. There is no matching puzzle underneath this — giving is how you find out who somebody is, not a lock to pick.
    • A pebble you keep is not a failure. You can carry one the whole time, or put it back down. Nothing notices.
    • Nobody steals anything. Real Adélie and gentoo penguins nick stones off each other constantly, which is funny and true and belongs in a different game. This one is about the locution.

    WHERE THIS COMES FROM, AND WHAT IT IS NOT

    Penguin pebbling is one of the Five Neurodivergent Love Locutions, coined by Amythest Schaber (Neurowonderful) and documented by Stimpunks in 2022. The line most people meet it through is @brainsandspoons’: “I thought about you today. I remembered this thing about you. Here’s something I want to share with you specifically.” Helen Edgar wrote it up at Autistic Realms in 2023: a pebble, a twig, a flower, a photograph from a walk — not spending money. There is a glossary entry too.

    There is already a Penguin Pebbling game, and this cabinet is not it. Penguin Pebbling is a thirty-card game by Helen Edgar (Autistic Realms) and Ryan Boren (Stimpunks), 2026, built around all five locutions. Go and play that one; it is the better thing and it is a real deck you can print. This cabinet takes nothing from it — no cards, no prompts, no artwork, no five-locution structure — and the reason is a licence rather than modesty: the deck is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and this site is CC BY-SA 4.0. An adaptation would carry the non-commercial clause onto a page that cannot hold it, and relicensing is not ours alone to do. So this is built on the locution, which belongs to the community.

    And one honest thing about the penguins. Adélie and gentoo penguins really do build nests out of stones and carry them about, and they really do steal them from each other. The tidy romance — one penguin choosing the perfect pebble and presenting it to its beloved — is the popular retelling rather than the finding. The locution is named after the image and is not weakened by that. A metaphor does not have to be a finding. It is said here rather than quietly traded on.

    THE DIAL AND THE SPEED KNOB

    These do different jobs. The dial governs the room — the glow behind the cabinets, the marquee bulbs. This cabinet’s speed knob governs the walking, and you hold that.

    The dial picks the knob’s starting position — Slow if you arrived on Gentle — and then stops having an opinion. All three speeds are here at every dial setting.
