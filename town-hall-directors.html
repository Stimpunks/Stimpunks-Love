Talking

The channel and the call in here are for moderators with the director role, and for administrators: moderators sign on at the Community Center with the moderators’ password, under a handle on the moderators’ list. Anybody can come in and look around, and the radio waits outside the door for everybody else.

This browser is not signed on as a moderator this room is for, so the call in here is not yours to join. Moderators sign on at the front desk.

The call is run by 8x8’s Jitsi as a Service, and the server hands a pass into it to the moderators this room is for and nobody else. Camera and microphone start off, and while somebody is in the call we keep their name in it, until they leave. What goes where.

Why frosted glass

Because the door is not locked and the room is not a secret. From the hall you can see that the light is on, and from in here you can see the hall is there: the glass lets the light through and keeps the detail back. The directors’ names are on this page for anybody to read, and what they say to each other in here stays in here. Nothing in the room moves, at any setting.
