A tree house in a grove

The Bough House

Jess’s, up among the autumn leaves, with the tree growing up through the middle of it.

The tree was here first. The house was built round it, so the trunk comes up through the floor and goes out through the roof, and two of its limbs curl round the walls as if they were holding it up, which they are. It is autumn up here: the leaves have turned gold, rust and crimson, a few are still green, and the ones that have let go lie deep round the foot of the ladder, and round a small fairy door at the foot of the trunk, the right size for whoever lives in a tree. The last of the afternoon sun is still in the top of the grove after the ground below has gone into shade.

Whose this is

This is Jess’s. She founded GROVE and runs it: mentoring, interest groups and a programme called BEING ME, for Autistic young people, by Autistic adults, based in the UK and open to young people anywhere. A tree house in a grove, for somebody whose work is called one.

Visit GROVE at gr0ve.org

Ben

Ben is a tiny black dog, and this is his tree house too. He is on the platform in the drawing, by the door.

One song, on repeat

There is one record up here and it plays on repeat: press it once and it goes round, from the top every time it ends, until you stop it. Nothing plays until you press.

Let Me Touch You For Awhile, by Alison Krauss & Union Station, written by Robert Lee Castleman, from their album New Favorite (2001). 3:23, on YouTube, via AlisonKraussVEVO.

Down the pebble paths

From the foot of the ladder a pebble path runs across the grass, past a pond with swing seats beside it, one of them a platform swing a wheelchair can roll onto, a hammock, and a playground for pets, to the Faery Yurt, and another runs on across the field to the Solarpunk Hermitage. The pond and the playground are on the campground between the pitches, and belong to nobody’s: every home stays its own.

The neighbours on the campgrounds, from the pond, down a pebble path or up the cable car: David Gray-Hammond’s Emergent Future Worlds Meets Mountain Man at the summit, Helen Edgar’s Faery Yurt at pitch 01 and Ryan Boren’s Solarpunk Hermitage at pitch 03. Go and visit.
