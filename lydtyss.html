Through the door marked E

Inside the SecrEt Cabin

Inside, everything is soft and slow.

Faery lights glow against the walls. Candles flicker. Fluffy blankets invite you to curl up close. A record player hums quietly in the background, while bottles of wine, coffee, weed, and an abundance of snacks make the cabin feel like somewhere you could melt into forever.

The record player

The rack beside it holds three records by Cigarettes After Sex: X’s, Cry, and Cigarettes After Sex. Each is the whole album, off the band’s own channel on YouTube, and every song on all three was opened on its own page on 2026-10-07 and plays, and may be played here. How long a record runs is its songs added up. The turntable takes one record at a time, and any song can be played on its own from the back of its sleeve. Nothing loads until you press one.

How it goes

Look for the door marked E. You know the password. Drift through the portal. Tend to the fire. You settle in, cosy and warm. Sip some wine or coffee. Snuggle under the soft blankets and let the world drift away ...

While you are here

  • The candles flicker, and never like a flash. At MAX on the dial they flicker by themselves; at the other settings they are lit and still until you press Let the candles flicker, and press it again to still them. A flame dims by a tenth of itself at most and changes slowly, which keeps every one well under what WCAG counts as a flash, and no two flicker together. The faery lights hold still, and at MAX drift a few pixels, slowly.
  • The radio has this room’s own channel, and keeps it in here. Signed on to the CB, your radio opens on This room in the cabin, for talking to whoever else is here. Be seen here, this room’s call, and a record or a film somebody hosts here are told only to the people in here: nobody on World or anywhere else on the street hears of them, Who’s online never lists this cabin, and a spot you add to a message here carries no address.
  • The record player and the door keep nothing. Not that you came, not what you played, and not the word.
  • Nobody here will ask you for anything. Not to explain yourself, not to join in, not to stay.

Whose it is

The words in this cabin are printed as they were given, and the cabin names nobody as their author. The records are Cigarettes After Sex’s, released on Partisan Records, and they play from the band’s own channel: nothing musical is hosted on this street, and not a line of any song is on this page. Each record’s year and label were read off MusicBrainz. The lights and the record player are Claude’s, drawn as code; How it’s made says how this street is built. Set in Comfortaa, drawn by Johan Aakerlund, and Figtree, drawn by Erik Kennedy, both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; both are on the street already, and the Foundry has them.
