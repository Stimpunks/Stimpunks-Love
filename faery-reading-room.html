The Reading Room

Every book I keep, a fire that has caught, and a chair that is yours while you are in it.

The yurt got too full of books, so they have a room of their own now, through the bookcase door. It is lit by the fire and by candles that never flicker, the walls are claret and the arches go up to points, and there is a sofa and two chairs turned to the hearth. Take any book off a shelf and it opens its author’s page. Nobody will ask what you are reading, or how far you have got.

HOW LOUD DO YOU WANT IT?

Starts wherever your device says. In here MAX turns the record on the gramophone; nothing else moves at any setting unless you ask it to, below, and the candles stand still.

Faeries drifting between the bookcases, and ivy and mushrooms growing very slowly round the edges of the room. Each stays still until you press it, whatever the dial says, and pressing again clears it. Nothing is stored.

The Bookcases

One case for each kind of book, alphabetical inside each by surname, and every spine a door: press one and it opens the author’s page on Wikipedia, or their own site where Wikipedia has none. Two of the cases are mine: what I have written and what has cited or carried it, and the ebooks and training packs at Autistic Realms. The last case is the artists whose paintings cannot hang in the gallery back in the yurt because their work is still their own.

Want to borrow a book rather than meet its author? Ryan lends his: every book on his shelves in the cave at the Solarpunk Hermitage, across the campgrounds, has a link to borrow it or read it.

The Gramophone

Something to read to. My library playlist is on the gramophone: switch it on and it runs from the top until you switch it off, or take one record off the shelf below and put that on instead. Nothing plays before you press.

Library Music, on YouTube, from my own channel.

Or take a record off the shelf: press its sleeve and it goes on the gramophone in place of the whole playlist and plays. Every sleeve says how long the record runs before you press it, nothing plays until you do, and taking it off puts the playlist back without starting it.
