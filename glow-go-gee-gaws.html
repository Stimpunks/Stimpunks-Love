Arlesglad Caverns, level B3

Glow Go Gee Gaws

Glow-in-the-dark toys and trinkets, in every colour glow comes in. Everything on these shelves is dark until you give it some light. Hold a thing up to the rail, take it away, and watch it give back what it kept.

The rail

The caverns’ handrail runs along the front of every shelf, and it is the only light in the shop. Hold a toy up to it and the toy drinks the light in; take it away and the toy gives it back, slowly, until it has none left. Nothing here makes a sound: the answer is the glow on the toy and the line of words under it.

and watch which colours let go first.

How long a glow lasts

Down here a glow lasts seconds, not the hours a real one can, so that you can watch one go from start to finish.

Green lasts longest, and red and orange go first, which is true of the real pigments. Green glow is the brightest and longest-lasting there is. Red and orange are the dimmest: partly because the pigments that glow those colours are weaker, and partly because your eyes are least sensitive to red in the dark. The order of the colours in between is ours, not measured.

The glow stick is the odd one out. It is not charged by light at all: bending it cracks a capsule inside, two liquids mix, and they glow until they are spent. It only goes once, so ours glows until you leave.

House rules

  • Nothing here is for sale. Trinkets or not, you play with them on the page.
  • Every colour glow comes in is on these shelves, blue included.
  • Nothing flickers or flashes, at any setting. A toy lights up at once when you charge it and fades once, smoothly. At Gentle it stays lit for its time and then goes dark in one step, and the words under it say exactly the same.
  • Nothing in here is radioactive. Glow pigment stores light you give it; it does not make its own.
  • Nothing is counted and nothing is kept. The shelves do not remember which toys you charged.

The toys, the drawings and every word about them are ours; the facts about how long each colour glows are credited in the liner notes.
