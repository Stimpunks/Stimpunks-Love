Spaceholder Saloon

For spaceholding, venting, solidarity, straight up bitchin’, and anything else those on spoon fumes want to bring and have a space to share or leave them here.

On the menu

Spaceholder Saloon only serves up patience, a listening ear, and a good ol’ fashioned root beer or mocktail.

  • Sarsaparilla

    The root beer’s rougher cousin, cold out of a brown bottle.

  • Hot cocoa

    As many marshmallows as the mug will hold, and then a couple more on the saucer.

  • A tall glass of ice water

    Free, and refilled before you notice it’s low.

  • A Shirley Temple

    Extra cherries, no charge, no comment.

  • Ginger beer

    Sharp enough to wake you up a little, if that’s what you’re after.

  • A covered bowl of pretzels

    Lid on, scoop beside it. Nobody’s hands have been in there.

  • Quiet, by the glass

    For when you’d rather not talk at all. Sit with it as long as you like.

Pull up a seat

  • The rocking chair

    Closest to the fire. Rock as much or as little as you want; the chair keeps time so you don’t have to.

  • The swing

    Hung from the rafters by the hearth. Feet up or feet down, still or swinging.

  • A table for one

    For when you want company in the room but not at your elbow. Nobody will sit down unless you wave them over.

  • The big table

    Room for everybody who turns up, and there’s always another chair to drag over.

Leave it here

There’s a row of hooks by the swinging doors. Hang up whatever you walked in carrying and leave it there as long as you like.

This saloon keeps no ledger. There’s nothing to fill in, and nothing you bring here is written down or sent anywhere.
