Fantasy Fancy Dress

Wings, ears, tails, paws, beads and dress-up suits, for your bodymind.

Behind the Dopamine Dress-Up Den, through a curtain, is a shop for dressing up: faery wings from small to enormous, animal ears on soft bands, tails, paws, bead necklaces, rings and bracelets, onesies, tiger, bear, dragon and unicorn suits, and a onesie made after one of your chimeras. Everything in here needs somebody to wear it, so it goes on a bodymind, the one you drew at the Bodymind Booth and brought through, and never on the Dress-Up Den’s valet stand. Every piece comes in every print and colourway the Dress-Up Den has, all black included, and nothing has to go with anything: a tiger suit with moth wings and bunny ears is a look.

Your strip

Every avatar you keep, at the Bodymind Booth or in the Dress-Up Den, goes into your Profile on the CB, a frame at a time. Signed on to the CB, you can open your strip here and bring a bodymind you kept into the fitting, with everything it had on. Change what you like, then take it back through to the Dress-Up Den and press Keep the changes there: it goes back knowing which one it is.

    House rules

    1. Everything goes on a bodymind. A tail or a pair of ears needs somebody to wear it, so nothing in here hangs on the Dress-Up Den’s stand. With nobody brought through, the fitting draws somebody at random, a different somebody every time, because no body is the one this shop starts from.
    2. Every piece says how it feels and how it goes on and comes off, the same as next door. Wings loop over the shoulders and go round nothing; ears sit on soft bands; tails clip to a waistband; paws leave the fingers free; every suit has a big zip pull you can find without looking.
    3. Wings fold flat for sitting back in a chair, and a tail lies to one side. Everything here is drawn on your bodymind as it is, on its feet or sitting down, and nothing put on here takes off anything you always have with you.
    4. Every piece comes in every print and every colourway the Dress-Up Den has, all black included, but one: a chimera onesie is in its chimera’s own colours, with the ears, tail, markings and back of the animals that gave it those, because it is made after that chimera. Each rail opens on a first suggestion, and that is all it is.
    5. Nothing has to go with anything. Dragon claws with a velvet choker and great faery wings is as good a look as any.
    6. Nothing in here is kept. Your bodymind came in through the address, or out of your strip, and goes back to the Dress-Up Den the same way, and keeping it as an avatar is done there, when you press Keep. Open my strip, signed on to the CB, only asks your Profile for the avatars you kept, and Bring my chimeras for the chimeras you kept in Chimera Obscura, each only when you press it.

    Whose this is

    This shop is Helen Edgar’s idea. She asked on 2026-10-15 for an annexe to the Dopamine Dress-Up Den, which was her idea too: faery wings big and small in every colour, jewellery, beads and rings, onesies, tiger, bear, dragon and unicorn outfits, and animal ears, tails and paws. She chose the name on the sign, and she chose that everything here is worn by your bodymind.

    The drawings are the house’s. Every piece is worked out again from the bodymind it is put on, its shoulders, middle, hips, height and how it gets about, so wings fit whoever wears them and a dragon tail lies the right way off a wheelchair seat.

    tools/make-fancy-dress.py builds these rails out of one data file and refuses rather than guesses. It will not hang a piece that does not say how it feels and how it comes off, a piece nothing can draw, or a word next door refuses, from sizes and gendered rails to fashion rules and scores, and it will not let this room’s own script keep or send anything.

    The type is Abril Fatface, by TypeTogether, for the sign, and Kumbh Sans, by Saurabh Sharma, for everything you read, both under the SIL Open Font License.
