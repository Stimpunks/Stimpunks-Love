A plant-based kitchen on the street

Vital Plant Living

Build your own bowl or wrap, or have one of ours.

On the shelf over the counter, cut through the middle like everything else in here: scallions, with their white bulbs showing under the soil; cilantro, lacy and many-lobed; garlic, a whole bulb in the soil with its cloves drawn in; ginger, tall and narrow-leaved over a knobbly rhizome; a chili plant with its red chilies hanging; carrots, feathery on top and one orange root going down; red potatoes, three of them in the soil under the leaves; flat Italian parsley, broader-leaved than the cilantro; a leek, its white shaft half under the soil; and a small lemon tree with lemons on it. Every pot on it is something in the pantry, and where the part the kitchen uses grows underground, you can see it under the soil.

Everything in here is plants, and everything in here is drawn the way a cook sketches a dish to show what is in it: cut straight through the middle, so you can see every layer sitting on the one under it. Build your own bowl or wrap out of the pantry below, or start from one of the combos on the board, each standing on a flavour base from somewhere in the world. The pantry is Ryan’s own, the list he cooks from at home, so these are a real kitchen’s shelves rather than a menu somebody imagined. When you have built something, copy it and take it shopping.

Where the name comes from

This room was going to be called Ital Plant Livity, and both of those words are Rastafari’s. Ital comes from vital with the v taken off, so that the word begins with I, which Rastafari speech does to a great many words; livity is the life Rastafari hold is in everybody. Rastafari has been a big influence on Ryan, and the playlist on the stereo is still called Ital Plant Livity and is full of songs about Ital food.

But this kitchen is not Ital, so it does not say it is. Many people who keep Ital avoid food with anything added to it, and some avoid added salt; this pantry has three kinds of salt, MSG, soy sauce and things out of packets. And how Ital is kept varies from one person to the next, which is not ours to settle from outside. So the name over the door says what it means in plain English, and the songs say the rest in their own words. Ital, on Wikipedia, which is where the lines above were checked.

Off the board

Named combos, each one built out of the same pantry and standing on one of the flavour bases Ryan wrote down: the aromatics a kitchen somewhere starts from. None of them is anybody’s grandmother’s recipe and none of them claims to be. Where a combo is thinking about a dish that is usually made with something this pantry does not keep, it says so rather than pretending.

  • Holy Trinity

    A bowl, on the Cajun Holy Trinity base, onion, celery, bell pepper

    Red beans and rice is what this bowl is thinking about, and that dish is cooked with red kidney beans. This pantry keeps pinto, so this is pinto, and the Cholula is on the table rather than in the pot.

    In it: Basmati rice, Pinto beans, Soy curls, Garlic, Scallions, Canola oil, Cholula, Paprika, Red pepper, Pepper, Hot peppers, Flat Italian parsley

  • Gold and Green

    A bowl, on the French Mirepoix, Italian Soffritto base, onion, celery, carrot

    Soft polenta under mushrooms in a marinara started on a soffritto, and the gremolata on top, which is lemon, garlic and parsley, and is the part you will remember.

    In it: Polenta, Mushrooms, Marinara, Olive oil, Garlic, Vegan butter, Nooch, Red pepper flakes, Gremolata, Pine nuts

  • Lentils, Slowly

    A bowl, on the French Mirepoix, Italian Soffritto base, onion, celery, carrot

    Lentils cooked slowly in a mirepoix, dressed while they are still warm with shallot, vinegar and mustard seed, on a heap of greens.

    In it: Lentils, Greens, Shallots, Olive oil, Red wine vinegar, Mustard seeds, Herbs de Provence, Pepper, Capers, Flat Italian parsley

  • Chickpeas and Spinach

    A bowl, on the Spanish Sofrito base, garlic, onion, tomato

    Chickpeas and spinach wilted into a sofrito with cumin and paprika, which is a tapa in Seville, with almonds on top because the texture step asks for something.

    In it: Garbanzo beans, Spinach, Olive oil, Paprika, Cumin seeds, Red wine vinegar, Almonds

  • Refogado Greens

    A bowl, on the Portuguese Refogado base, onions, garlic, peppers, tomatoes

    Potatoes and a great many greens cooked down in a refogado, the onion, garlic, pepper and tomato a Portuguese kitchen starts from, with olives.

    In it: Golden potatoes, Greens, Garbanzo beans, Olive oil, Paprika, Olives, Sea salt flakes

  • Kraut and Potatoes

    A bowl, on the German Suppengrün base, carrots, leeks, celeriac

    Red potatoes and seared tempeh with sauerkraut and pickles, on Suppengrün. The celeriac comes with that base; the pantry does not keep it otherwise.

    In it: Red potatoes, Tempeh, Sauerkraut, Mustard seeds, Dill pickles, Vegan butter, Apple cider vinegar, Pepper, Flat Italian parsley

  • Paprika Potatoes

    A bowl, on the Hungarian base, paprika, fat, onion

    Paprika, fat and onion are the whole of the Hungarian base, and the fat here is vegan butter. A spoon of sour cream on top and it is done.

    In it: Red potatoes, Soy curls, Bell peppers, Vegan butter, Paprika, Vegan sour cream, Pepper, Flat Italian parsley

  • Garlic Ginger Scallion

    A bowl, on the Chinese base, garlic, ginger, scallions

    A stir-fry on the three aromatics Ryan wrote down for Chinese cooking, and a spoon of corn starch in water at the end, which is what makes the sauce shine and hold on.

    In it: Jasmine rice, Tofu, Broccoli, Napa cabbage, Snow peas, Soy sauce, Sesame oil, Rice vinegar, Hoisin, Corn starch + liquid, 5 spice, Water chestnuts, Sesame seeds

  • Red Oil Tofu

    A bowl, on the Sichuan base, garlic, ginger, chili peppers

    Hot rather than numbing, and it says so: the numbing comes from Sichuan pepper, which this pantry does not keep. Silken or firm tofu, whichever you have in.

    In it: Jasmine rice, Tofu, Mushrooms, Pepper-infused oil, Soy sauce, Rice vinegar, Red pepper flakes, 5 spice, Scallions, Peanuts

  • Green Curry, Green Everything

    A bowl, on the Thai curry base, shallots, garlic, chiles, lemongrass

    Green curry paste let down with coconut milk on the curry base Ryan wrote down, with every green thing the pantry has that will hold up in it.

    In it: Jasmine rice, Tofu, Green curry paste, Coconut milk, Snap peas, Bell peppers, Spinach, Lime juice, Cilantro, Peanuts

  • Hand Roll

    A wrap, in nori, on the Japanese base, mirin, soy sauce

    Sushi rice is rice seasoned with vinegar, which is why the rice vinegar is here. Rolled in nori by hand, a cone rather than a log, and eaten straight away while the nori still snaps.

    In it: Sushi rice, Edamame, Avocado, Pickled veggie slaw, Rice vinegar, Sesame seeds, Scallions

  • Lemon, Oil, Oregano

    A wrap, in pita, on the Greek base, lemon juice, olive oil, oregano

    Potatoes roasted in lemon, olive oil and oregano, which is the Greek base doing all the work, in a pita with olives, pepperoncini and a spoon of sour cream.

    In it: Golden potatoes, Greens, Tomato, Red onions, Olives, Pepperoncini, Vegan sour cream

  • The Falafel Pocket

    A wrap, in pita, on the Lebanese base, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil

    Falafel in a pita with hummus and babaganoush both, because nobody made you choose, and pickles for the crunch that falafel wants beside it.

    In it: Falafel, Hummus, Tahini, Babaganoush, Tomato, Greens, Pickled red onion, Dill pickles, Flat Italian parsley

  • Rice and Peas, Near Enough

    A bowl, on the Jamaican base, garlic, scallion, thyme; and Ryan’s own go-to, coconut milk, rice, beans

    Rice and peas is cooked with kidney beans or gungo peas, coconut milk, thyme and scallion. This pantry keeps pinto, so it is near enough, and says so. Laurel Aitken's Rice and Peas is on the stereo.

    In it: Basmati rice, Pinto beans, Coconut milk, Greens, Allspice, Hot peppers, Lime juice, Mango

  • Groundnut

    A bowl, on the West African base, chili peppers, onions, tomatoes

    Groundnut stews are cooked across West Africa under a lot of names. This is a bowl thinking about them, on Ryan's West African base, with peanut butter stirred into the tomato until it goes the colour of the wall in here.

    In it: Jasmine rice, Golden potatoes, Greens, Peanut butter, Tomato, Peanuts

  • Three Chiles Burrito

    A wrap, in burrito tortillas, on the Mexican pepper base, ancho, pasilla, guajillo

    Ancho, pasilla and guajillo are the three chiles, and rice, beans and a flour burrito tortilla are Ryan's own line. The rest is everything that fits before it will not roll.

    In it: Basmati rice, Black beans, Vegan textured protein, Salsa, Guacamole, Pico de gallo, Vegan cheese, Vegan sour cream, Cilantro, Lime juice, Cumin seeds, Smoked salt (Chihuahua de Mexico)

  • The Go-To

    A bowl, on Ryan’s own go-to, tahini, tamari, sriracha

    Tahini, tamari and sriracha is Ryan's own go-to, and it is the dressing here: stir the three together and pour it over whatever is in the bowl. It goes on most things.

    In it: Quinoa, Tofu, Greens, Broccoli, Tahini, Tamari, Sriracha, Scallions, Sesame seeds, Hemp seeds, hearts

  • Black Salt Scramble

    A wrap, in flour tortillas, on Ryan’s own go-to, MSG, black salt, nooch

    Crumbled tofu, gone yellow with turmeric, on Ryan's MSG, black salt and nooch. Black salt smells of eggs, which is the reason it is in there, and the potatoes are left over from last night.

    In it: Tofu, Golden potatoes, Spinach, Bell peppers, Turmeric, Salsa, Hot peppers

House rules

  • Everything in here is plants. Every bowl, every wrap, everything at the counter.
  • Everything touches. The sauce runs into the rice and that is the idea. If you need your food kept apart, Samefood Cafe is a few doors along, and nothing on a plate in there touches anything.
  • Vital means alive. Nobody in here will tell you what a bowl does for your body, and nothing on the board is good for you or bad for you. It is food.
  • Nothing is weighed and nothing is counted: no portions, no numbers on anything, no favourite of the week.
  • The builder keeps nothing and sends nothing. Copying puts your ticket on your own clipboard and nowhere else, and closing the page empties the bowl.
  • No claims about anybody’s cooking. A flavour base is where a kitchen somewhere starts from; what you build on it is yours.
  • Hot peppers, everything. Nooch, everything. Those two are Ryan’s, and they are on the house.

Whose these are

The pantry is Ryan’s, the list he cooks from for himself, written down as he gave it, including his names for its sections and his list of pairings, trinities and go-tos, which is where every flavour base in the builder comes from. Where a line of his held several things it is split into them, and a thing on his list twice is in the builder once. The combos, the notes on them and the drawings are ours. The words Ital and livity are Rastafari’s, and the songs on the stereo belong to the musicians named beside them.

tools/make-vital.py builds this room out of one data file. It refuses a combo that brings in anything the pantry does not keep, and a combo that stands on no flavour base. It refuses the word Ital anywhere on the menu. It refuses the vocabulary of authenticity, of wellness and of counting in the room’s own voice. And it refuses a pot on the shelf that is not on the menu, and anything you can tick that the drawing of your bowl would not know how to draw.

The type is Bagel Fat One, by Kyungwon Kim and JAMO, for the kitchen’s name and its headings, and Be Vietnam Pro, by Lâm Bảo, Tony Le and ViệtAnh Nguyễn, for everything you read: open, round-shouldered and easy going down a long list, which is most of this page.
