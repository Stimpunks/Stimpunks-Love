What an excursion is

An excursion is part room, part announcement and part run of show. It is somewhere to go for one gathering, with the room drawn round it; it says when and where and what to bring; and it carries the way round on the night, the questions in order and the ways out of every one of them. Everybody who comes can read the whole of it beforehand, so nothing on the night is a surprise.

After the night it stays on the rack, as a record of where we went, so anybody who could not come can take the same walk round on their own, any time, at their own pace.

None of them is a room on the street, and none of them is ours to restyle: each is its maker’s, in their colours and their faces, with the street’s dial, sign-off and radio arriving as they do everywhere.

Bring one

If you are making something for one of our gatherings and would like it on the rack, tell us on the CB or in the forums, or in the Community Center. It comes in with your name on it, under the street’s licence, CC BY-SA 4.0, and with your say on how it looks. A link to a business of your own may go in it, signed as one.

The room is ours, drawn and written as code by Claude for Stimpunks. Every excursion on the rack is its maker’s, and its page says so. Type is Josefin Sans, by Santiago Orozco, and Public Sans, by the United States Web Design System, Dan Williams, Pablo Impallari and Rodrigo Fuenzalida.
