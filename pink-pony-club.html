Room two is a dancefloor. Every song we have ever hung on a page lives in here, in one jukebox, with the credits attached to it rather than buried in a footer.

HOW LOUD DO YOU WANT IT?

Starts wherever your device says. Nothing moves, flashes, or plays until you say so.

NOTHING PLAYS UNTIL YOU PRESS PLAY.

No autoplay, ever, in any room — and the buttons below are not players yet. Until you press one, nothing on this page has contacted YouTube: no iframe, no cookie, no request. A site about consent that grabs your speakers is just another thing being done to you.

ROOM RULE

Pink Pony Club is the vibe, not the lyrics. We name the artist, link the song, and let them keep their own words. Nothing here is hosted by us and nothing here is ours.

THERE IS A DOOR AT THE BACK OF THIS ROOM

It opens onto The Chappell — a rhinestone Vatican holding thirteen Chappell Roan videos, under the heading Unabashed Pleasure and Righteous Inclusion. It looks nothing like this room, and that is not an accident. Every button in there that plays one thing says how long it runs before you press it — and the one at the east end, which plays the whole playlist, says instead that it runs until you stop it.
