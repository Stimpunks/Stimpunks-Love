The Campfire

The Campfire is for small groups and teams working on something together or watching/listening together.

How it goes here

Campfire Norms

  • Video optional.
  • Mute when you’re not actively talking.
  • Quietly lurking with camera and mic off is perfectly fine and accepted.

The fire

The fire in the middle is the fire pit at Swaying Sweetgrass’s own playlist of real campfires, read out of that room’s list, and it runs until you stop it. The fires round it are from the same playlist, one at a time, each with how long it runs. Play one here, or put it on the fire; each is yours, at your own pace. In the call, anybody can put a YouTube video on for everybody from the call’s own menu, under Share video, and then whoever shares it plays and pauses it for everybody, which is watching together the way a campfire does it.

Talking

The call in here is for people signed on to the CB. Anybody can come in and look around; to talk, and to be in the call, you need the CB, and the Community Center says how to get on it.

You are not signed on to the CB in this browser. Sign on at the Community Center, then come back and the way in is here.

A Note on Communication Access. We support multiple modes of participation including text chat, audio, and video — whatever works for your communication needs on a given day. You do not need to speak to belong here.

The call is run by 8x8’s Jitsi as a Service. Your camera and microphone start off, the name you are called by goes to 8x8 inside your pass into the call, and we keep nothing about a call. What goes where.

Why a campfire

People have sat round a fire to work on something together, and to watch and listen together, for as long as there have been people. Everybody faces the same way, which is in, and nobody is at the head of it. Sit on a log, keep your camera and microphone off if you like, and listen. Nothing in this room moves, at any setting, the flame included.
