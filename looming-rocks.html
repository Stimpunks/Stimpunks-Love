Turning 03, and you can hear it from the road

Looming Rocks Amphitheatre

Two rock faces, a stage between them, and a great deal of very old stone that was here long before the stage was.

Set amidst towering rock faces, Looming Rocks impose with their immense solidity. They give off an ancient and alive aura. Some say the rocks are sentient and full of lore from centuries of hosting storytellers and stages. They possess an unknowable intelligence that long pre-dates the humans drawn to them. The rocks saw the Sithen emerge, eons ago.

Why the rocks glow

The rocks are covered in lichen. In modern times, folks discovered that UV light made them fluoresce in a dazzling display. Charged during the day by a solar array with batteries, the stage production at Looming Rocks includes UV spots and floods that make the rocks glow at night.

The floods stand on the ground and point upwards, which is why everything out here is brightest at its foot and goes dark towards the top, and why there is nothing overhead at all. The lamps are violet. What comes back is not.

The door policy at the foot of the rocks

  • Tuning is not playing. While the stage is dark, previous and next are silent — the screen names the act and its runtime and nothing is fetched. You can walk the whole bill without a single request leaving the page. Once something is on, the desk changes the picture instead.
  • Every control says how long before you press it, and the desk names the act it is about to move to as well. The shortest thing here is nearly an hour. Nobody should find out the length of a thing after committing to it.
  • The bill is a list and not a verdict. Nothing here is ranked, rated or counted. Six shows in an order, and the order is not an argument about which is better.
  • Nothing in the rig flashes or strobes, at any dial setting. A venue is exactly the kind of room that would grow a lighting effect, and this street settled that question in the punk club: a light that can put somebody on the floor has no business in a room anybody is meant to enjoy. The rocks glow steadily or not at all.
  • You are allowed to sit down and you are allowed to leave. There is no standing, no queue and no crowd here, which is the one thing a web page can genuinely offer that the real venue cannot — and the reason a room like this is worth having on a Disabled people’s site rather than being a poster of somewhere most of us would struggle to get round.

Whose this all is

The real venue is Red Rocks Amphitheatre, an open-air amphitheatre near Morrison, Colorado, about ten miles south-west of Denver, owned and operated by the city of Denver. It is formed by two enormous sandstone monoliths — they have names: Ship Rock and Creation Rock, with the smaller Stage Rock between them — and it seats 9,525. Read off Wikipedia’s article on it on 2026-09-22. Every recording on this page was made there.

Looming Rocks is not Red Rocks. It is a room on a made-up street that is extremely fond of the real place — our executive director goes whenever she can — and Stimpunks has no connection to the venue, is not endorsed by it, and is not describing it. The amphitheatre in Colorado is a real thing that belongs to a city; this one is a room on a street that rearranges itself for whoever turns up.

None of these artists, their labels or the channels hosting these recordings is affiliated with Stimpunks, and none of them endorses us. The full running order with channels and links is in the liner notes.

The job: What the rocks give back

Before the code: The rocks at Looming Rocks glow under the UV floods, and the room is careful that the stone itself is not what glows. What is?

Hint 1

It is not the stone and it is not paint. It is alive, and it is growing on the rock.

Hint 2

It is the thing a walker would call a crust or a patch on a boulder.

Last hint

Fungus and alga living as one organism.

Give me the answer

The answer is The lichen. The lichen. Many lichens fluoresce under ultraviolet light — they take light you cannot see and give back light you can — and it is a real technique for telling species apart in the field. The rig, the solar array and the sentient rocks are this street's invention; the fluorescence is not. The room separates those two halves in the same paragraph, and its tool refuses to name any particular species, because nobody here has checked which one glows what and a confident Latin name beside a colour is the format most likely to be believed.

Taking the answer is a real way through this, not a lesser one. Nothing is watching and nothing is written down.
