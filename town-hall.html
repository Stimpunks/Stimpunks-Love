Town Hall

The street’s town hall, at noon. It is one round room under a dome, and the top of the dome is open to the sky, so the sun comes down through the hole and lies on the floor as a single disc that moves round the hall through the day. This is where Stimpunks meets to run itself, and the doors round the wall go to the rooms where that happens. Every one of them is open to walk into. What changes from door to door is who can talk once you are in.

The doors round the wall

  • The Fishbowl A room for community Fishbowl Meetings for anyone with CB access. Anybody can walk in, and anybody signed on to the CB can talk there. go in →
  • Directors A room for Stimpunks Directors to meet. Anybody can walk in. The radio and the call in there are for the base, signed on with the moderators’ password. not open yet
  • Board A room for Stimpunks Board Members to meet. Anybody can walk in. The radio and the call in there are for the base, signed on with the moderators’ password. not open yet
  • Moderators A room for Stimpunks Moderators to meet. Anybody can walk in. The radio and the call in there are for the base, signed on with the moderators’ password. not open yet

Who can talk where

  • Anybody can walk into any room here, signed on or not, and read everything on its walls.
  • The Fishbowl is the CB’s. Anybody signed on at the Community Center can tune the radio to it and join its call.
  • The rooms for our directors, our board and our moderators are the base’s. The base is whoever signed on with the moderators’ password, and in those rooms the radio only comes in with them: for everybody else it waits outside the door, and is there again in the hall.
  • That is a lock and not a curtain. The street’s server hands those rooms’ channel, their call and any film hosted in them to the base and to nobody else, whatever a browser asks for, and nobody else’s radio hears that anything is going on in there.
  • The house norms are the CB’s, at the Community Center, and our covenant applies in every room of this building.

Why a hole in the roof

Some of what gets said in a town hall has to stay in the room it was said in, and the rooms round this wall have doors for that reason. The hall in the middle is open to the sky. Our bylaws are published in full: our accountability pages on stimpunks.org carry the bylaws and a transparency log. A private conversation and a public outcome are two different things, and this building has a place for each.
