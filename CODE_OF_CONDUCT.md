# Code of conduct

**Our code of conduct is the [Stimpunks Covenant](https://stimpunks.org/covenant/).** It holds for
everybody here: Stimpunks' own people, anybody sending a pull request from a fork, and anybody on
the street's CB. Stimpunks Foundation names it as its code of conduct in its
[Whistleblower & Concern Reporting Policy](https://stimpunks.org/about/accountability/reporting-concerns/#h-this-policy-and-the-covenant),
and it takes in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which every Stimpunk agrees to.

This file does not restate the covenant. It is kept up to date on stimpunks.org, and a copy here
would drift. What this file adds is where the covenant applies in this repository, the few things
this project asks on top of it, and how to tell somebody when something goes wrong.

In brief, from the covenant itself:

> - We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an **open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community**.
> - Don’t be **TRAAAAASH** (transphobic, racist, ableist, abusive, anti-Black, anti-Indigenous, anti-Semitic, sexist, homophobic).
> - In addition to speaking different languages, we have different neurotypes with different communication styles and norms of sociality. In the case of misunderstanding, assume good intention.
> - Maintain learner safety and remember what it is like to be a new contributor.
>
> — [Stimpunks Covenant](https://stimpunks.org/covenant/#h-in-brief)

## Where it applies

Everywhere this project happens: issues, pull requests, reviews, commit messages and discussions on
[this repository](https://github.com/Stimpunks/Stimpunks-Love) and on forks of it; everything on
[stimpunks.world](https://stimpunks.world/) that people write to each other, the CB's channels,
direct messages, calls, forums, the suggestion box and the boards included; and anywhere somebody
speaks for the street.

## What this project asks on top of the covenant

- **Credit people.** Attribution is the habit this street kept when it dropped the others. Name
  whose work, words, drawing or idea something is, and never relicense what is not ours: the
  [licence](LICENSE) says what is ours to give.
- **Ask before you bring somebody in.** A photograph of a person, a recording of a voice, or
  somebody's own work goes up only with their yes, and comes down when they ask, once, without a
  conversation. [The polaroid wall](https://stimpunks.world/polaroids.html) says how.
- **Keep people's details out of the repository.** Everything here is public the moment it is
  pushed, and a commit is hard to take back: it stays in every copy and fork made before it was
  removed. No names, addresses, diagnoses or messages of anybody who did not put them here
  themselves.
- **Ask the calls that are somebody else's, never make them.** Some things on the street are a
  particular person's to decide, Helen Edgar's Faery Yurt for one. A pull request asks; it does
  not decide for them. [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) says which calls are whose.
- **Being new is fine, and so is being slow.** Nobody is waiting. A question is a contribution,
  and nobody here is marked on how they ask it.

## When something goes wrong

**Most friction is ordinary**, and the covenant has a way through it: an advice process,
restorative practices and transformative justice, in its section on
[prosocial decision making and conflict resolution](https://stimpunks.org/covenant/#h-prosocial-decision-making-and-conflict-resolution).
Talking it over is usually the first step, and you never have to do that with somebody who has
harmed you.

**To reach somebody privately:**

- **On the CB**, write `@moderator` to call our moderators, or use **Report** on a direct message,
  which puts that message on the moderators' desk. The [privacy page](https://stimpunks.world/privacy.html#moderator-calls)
  says what a call and a report keep.
- **About this repository, or anything else**, email **stimpunks@stimpunks.org**, or write to any
  member of the board, listed on [Board & Governance](https://stimpunks.org/about/accountability/board-governance/).
  Please do not open an issue about a person: issues here are public the moment they are opened.
- **About somebody who runs the street, Ryan Boren or Helen Edgar included**, go to the board. You
  never have to route a concern through the person it is about.
- **For misconduct, discrimination, abuse, danger to somebody, or a pattern that talking has not
  stopped**, use the [Whistleblower & Concern Reporting Policy](https://stimpunks.org/about/accountability/reporting-concerns/).
  You can report anonymously, you are protected from retaliation even if you turn out to be
  mistaken, and we acknowledge a concern within five business days.
- **A security problem** is not a conduct report: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says where to send it.

## What we may do

The people who look after this repository (Ryan Boren and Helen Edgar) and our moderators on the CB
may take down a comment, a message, a post or a contribution that breaks the covenant, lock a
conversation, and stop somebody taking part, for a while or for good: on GitHub by blocking them
from the repository, on the CB by a kick, a time out or a ban, as the
[privacy page](https://stimpunks.world/privacy.html#bars) describes. A time out or a ban on the CB
keeps until when it holds and nothing else: not which moderator made it, and not why. We start from
repair rather than punishment wherever that is safe.

Taking something down from GitHub does not take it out of every copy somebody has already made.
We will say so plainly rather than promise more than we can do.
