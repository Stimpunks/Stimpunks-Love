What the strengths mean

  • A percentage is how much of the drink is alcohol, by volume. It is on every pour, before you take it, the way every button on this street says how long it runs before you press it.
  • Alcohol-free, for a beer or a wine, means under 0.05%. That is the same drink with the alcohol taken out after it was made, and a brewed drink with the alcohol taken out can keep the faintest trace of it. If you need none at all, the kombucha’s alcohol-free pours have none.
  • Kombucha is never quite zero, and ours says so. It is fermented, so there is always a little alcohol in it, and how much varies from one batch to the next. So its fermented pour gives the most it can be, under 0.5%, rather than a number that would be wrong half the time. Its alcohol-free pour is the same tea and fruit, not fermented.

House rules

  • Nobody here asks why you are not drinking, or whether you are sure, or whether you want to try a little of the other one.
  • Nothing here counts how many you have had. No rounds, no games, nobody keeping up with anybody.
  • The pours with alcohol in them are for adults. Everything else in the cellar is for everybody.
  • Nothing here is good for you, and nothing is bad for you either. None of it is a remedy or a supplement, kombucha included. It is something to drink.
  • The vessels are glass and steel on bare rock, lit from behind, so the light comes through the drink. There is no wood in here.

The house styles, their names and every word about them are ours. The cellar videos are their makers’, on their own channels, and none of them is affiliated with Stimpunks. Credits are in the liner notes.
