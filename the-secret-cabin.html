Off the usual paths

The SecrEt Cabin

Tucked away on the outskirts, beyond the usual paths, there is a cabin waiting in the woods, behind a wooden door marked with the letter E.

The letter is more than a sign. E holds the Element needed to pass through the portal — an invitation into a space of love, longing, yearning, and fulfilling exploration of bodyminds and entangled ways of being.

The password

You know the password. Say it to the door, in capitals or not, and it opens.

This browser cannot check the word for the door. The word is also the way in: put it after stimpunks.world/ in the address bar yourself.

Before you go in

  • It is a room for grown-ups. There is wine and weed on the shelves, and it is a place for desire, kink and closeness. Nobody in there will ask you to do anything.
  • Nothing plays until you press it. There is a record player inside, and it waits for you.
  • The candles inside flicker, and so does their light through the door at MAX on the dial: gently, by a tenth at most, well under what counts as a flash.
  • This door is listed nowhere. Not on the map, the teleporter, the search, the sitemap, the feeds or the changelog. You found it because somebody gave you the address, and the way to bring somebody else is to do the same.
  • The door keeps nothing. Not the word you said, not a wrong one, and not how many times you tried.

Whose it is

The words on this door are printed as they were given, and the cabin names nobody as their author. The door, the woods and the light through the E are Claude’s, drawn as code; How it’s made says how this street is built. Set in Cinzel, drawn by Natanael Gama, and Hanken Grotesk, drawn by Alfredo Marco Pradil, Hanken Design Co., both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; both are on the street already, and the Foundry has them. The E cut in the door is Great Vibes’s capital, drawn by Robert Leuschke, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: the face is not loaded here, only that one letter, taken as an outline and cut.
