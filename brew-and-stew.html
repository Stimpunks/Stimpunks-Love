A woodland tea room

Brew and Stew

Herbal teas, soups and stews, and bread, in a cabin in the woods. Everything on the board is vegan.

A cabin at the edge of the trees, and nobody lit it. The light grows in the wood: a pale glow along the joints between the logs, in the moss on the sill and in the little mushrooms at the foot of the walls, enough to read by and never enough to throw a shadow. The stove is shut iron, so it gives the room its warmth and none of its light. There is no flame in here anywhere, and nothing in here moves, at any setting: you come in off a dark field into a room that is lit by itself, and sit down.

In the tea room

The board

Everything on the board is vegan, and every card says what is in it and which of the nine major food allergens the FDA names it has, and gluten. Every tea is a tisane, made from flowers, leaves, roots, seeds or fruit and none of the tea plant, so none of them has any caffeine. There is no honey, so every tea comes with maple syrup on the side.

Nothing on the board is a remedy. A cup of chamomile here is a cup of chamomile, and nothing on a card says what it will do for you. Nothing was picked in the wood either: the herbs come from the cottage garden and the jars on the shelf, and the mushrooms in the soup were grown on a farm. Everything is made at the one stove, so if an allergy is serious, say so there, and somebody will tell you what has been near what.

House rules

  • Sit as long as you like. Nobody clears a table that somebody is still at.
  • Bring the arrangement back to the shelf when you go, for whoever comes in next.
  • No flame. The stove stays shut, and nobody brings a light in: the wood does that.
  • Nothing in here makes a sound, and nothing moves, at any setting.
  • Nobody is counted: not the cups, not the bowls, and not you.

What the tea room keeps

  • Nothing about you. What is on your table lives in this page and nowhere else, and is gone when you leave it. The tea room’s own script stores nothing in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. A film in the looking-glass loads from YouTube only when you press it.
  • Every card says what is in it before you ask for it, and every tea says it has no caffeine.
  • Nothing plays until you press play, and every film says how long it runs first.
