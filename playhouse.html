HOUSE RULES

Every toy is a real button. Tab reaches it, a screen reader names it, and it works without a mouse. A playhouse you cannot get into is a diorama.

Sound is on press only. The room is silent when you walk in and stays silent if you never touch anything. The noises are synthesised here, not fetched from anywhere.

Gentle mode keeps the toys. It takes away the wobble and the confetti, not the things to press.
