Words, Bud, Beats

Kept by Kristina Brooke (she/her)

What's here

An intimate salon of words, highlighted by the aroma of fragrant bud, and the music whose volume seems to be just right. The words, the music, the strain are more or less a record of the moment. Some things make sense. Others make noise.

Take a look around; enjoy the knickknacks and the plants. It's not cluttered, but it comes close. Every item has a story. A history. A place. The microphone is decorative and that's OK. Talking is never required.

House notes

This space is free from all of the nine, and gluten.

Links to single songs on Tidal will allow you to play the song on your choice of platform. Choose whichever you prefer. They will play on an external site.

Videos in this room wait for you. Nothing plays until you press it, and you can stop it whenever you like.

Open mic

Bring a pairing of your own. Fill in the slip, press Copy, and keep it or share it wherever you like. Nothing here is sent or saved. If you are not sure of a line, say so rather than guess.

Pairing slip

  1. 30 or fewer. No song lyrics.
  2. The person who wrote them, not who passed them to you. Your own name if they are yours.
  3. With chapter or section, if you know it.
  4. Edition, year, publisher.
  5. The work itself, not a quotation site.
  6. Public domain, a Creative Commons licence, all rights reserved, or your own words.
  8. The song or playlist, who made it, a link, and how long it runs.
  10. In your own words.
  11. As you want it credited, and where it links, or none.

Adapted from the attribution slip in the Stimpunks.World Zibaldone.
