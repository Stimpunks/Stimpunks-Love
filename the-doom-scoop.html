The Doom Scoop

Daily news, usually bad.

Every morning a timer reads the channels Ryan keeps an eye on and fills a tub in the cabinet with what they put out between five the morning before and five that morning. That is the morning edition. The newest stands at the front with the scoop in it; the six mornings before it are behind it, each a flavour of its own and each a little more scraped out than the last.

A week, and then it is gone. The eighth morning is not kept, not archived and not moved to a back page. It goes, and it is still on the channel that posted it. Up the street is the Doomscroll, whose whole shape is a roll with no end to it. This one has a bottom, and you can see it from the door.

The cabinet was last filled at 5:16 am on Saturday 17 October, Mountain time, and the newest tub is that morning’s.

Behind the counter

  • Nothing plays until you press it. Nothing reaches YouTube until you press a scoop, and pressing one plays that video and no other.
  • Every tub has a screen of its own at the top. Any video in that tub plays where it is, or goes up on the screen, bigger, with the second button under it. Taking it off leaves the screen empty again.
  • Every scoop says how long it runs before you press it, when it went up and whose channel it is on. A short is the shape of a phone, because that is the shape it plays in.
  • Nobody here watched any of it before it went up. A timer filled the cabinet. What it asked about every video is whether it plays, whether it can be shown on this site and how long it runs. A video whose channel will not let it be shown on another site, or that YouTube shows only to signed-in adults, is a door out to YouTube rather than a screen in here, and it says which.
  • The headlines are the channels’ own, word for word and capital for capital, and some of them shout. Rewriting somebody’s headline would be putting words in their mouth.
  • Nothing is ranked and nothing is counted. No views, no likes, nothing trending and no most-watched. The sources are in the groups Ryan put them in and the order he listed them, and each one’s scoops are in the order they went up.
  • A feed only reaches so far back. A channel’s feed carries its newest uploads and nothing older, so a channel that posts a great deal between two mornings has the earliest of them left off. Where that has happened, the tub says so on that channel rather than looking complete.
  • Every time is Mountain time, the street’s own clock.

On the counter

Ground News and MeidasTouch News publish no feed a timer can read, so nothing is scooped from them. One answers “not found” wherever a feed usually lives and the other puts up a bot check, and a bot check is somebody saying no to a machine. They are doors on the counter instead, to their own front pages as they are today.

The job: What flavour is Thursday?

Before the code: Every tub in the cabinet is a morning, and every morning is a flavour. What flavour is Thursday?

Hint 1

Every tub's lid says its day and its flavour, right under the date.

Hint 2

Find the tub whose date is a Thursday; there is always exactly one in a week.

Last hint

The plainest flavour there is.

Give me the answer

The answer is Vanilla. Every morning's tub in the cabinet is a flavour, and the flavour is the day of the week, so Thursday is always vanilla whatever is in it. How far down a tub is scraped says how many mornings old it is, and after a week it is gone.

Taking the answer is a real way through this, not a lesser one. Nothing is watching and nothing is written down.
