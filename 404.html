Nothing at this address

The number is on the post and there is no house behind it. Whatever the link promised is not on this street — not hidden, not behind a door, just not here.

Why that might be

  • A letter went missing on the way, or one too many came with it. Capitals are forgiven here and so is a stray space; a dropped letter is not, and neither is the full stop or bracket a link picks up when it is copied out of the end of a sentence.
  • It is somewhere else now. The changelog is a dated record of what changed on the street, and it is the place to look when something you remember has gone.
  • It was never on this street. We publish several sites and some of their addresses look like these. The garden has a bed for every one of them, each linking straight out.

Ways out

  • The street, where every door is.
  • The garden, through the gate, for our other sites.
  • Tell a person what you were looking for, if a link of ours sent you here.

Fog lights everything from every side at once, so nothing here casts a shadow and nothing has a lit face. The only way to tell near from far is how pale a thing has gone, which is why the plate is the only thing drawn close enough to read.
