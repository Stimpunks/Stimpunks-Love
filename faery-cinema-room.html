Behind the Faery Yurt · through the cinema door

The Cinema

Velvet curtains, a silver screen, and every film I keep on a reel of its own.

Come in out of the yurt and sit anywhere you like. There is no programme and no time it starts: the films are on reels along the rails on the wall, in rows, and whichever one you take down goes up on the screen. The projector in the box at the back hums to itself and never flickers, the candles stand still, and nothing plays until you press it.

HOW LOUD DO YOU WANT IT?

Starts wherever your device says. In here MAX turns the reels on the projector; nothing else moves unless you ask it to, below, and the light from the box never flickers.

Faeries drifting through the projector’s light, and ivy and mushrooms growing very slowly round the edges of the room. Each stays still until you press it, whatever the dial says, and pressing again clears it. Nothing is stored.

The Reels

The screen. Nothing is showing. Any reel on the rails below can go up here, and nothing loads until you press one.

Take a reel off the rail: press it and it goes up on the screen and plays. Every reel says how long the film runs before you press it. A whole channel or playlist says it runs until you stop it, and plays from the top with the rest to scroll through in the player; a live camera says it runs until you close it. Nothing plays until you press it, and taking it off leaves the screen dark again.
