WHAT THIS STREET IS FOR

Every other site we run is careful on purpose — careful palettes, careful sourcing, careful attribution, careful hedging. Those sites are load-bearing and they stay. This one is the other half of the same lung.

It has no single design system, deliberately. The street is the system; the rooms refuse to share one. And it has edges — the garden through the gate in the wall, where the sites we publish grow, and the campgrounds past the treeline, for anybody who would rather not be on a street at all. Two things stay careful even here: attribution, because that is a licence and not a house style, and contrast, because clashing is not the same as illegible. Everything else went out the window.
